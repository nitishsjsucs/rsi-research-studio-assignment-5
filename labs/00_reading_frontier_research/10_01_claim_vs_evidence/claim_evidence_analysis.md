# Claim vs evidence: the three papers this repo rebuilds

Generated by `lab.py`. Each claim is paired with the experiment that supports it and the conditions that limit it.


## Budget RSI: scoring code edits before they run (arXiv:2610.29121)

| # | Claim | Supporting evidence (as summarised) | What remains untested |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A linear readout over parent rate + 8 diff coordinates picks better edits than ignoring the diff | HumanEval+ with GPT-4o: 87.0% held-out vs 84.2% for the parent-rate ablation | other models, other benchmarks, edits not shaped like the 96 training edits |
| 2 | Scoring before running cuts evaluation cost by roughly an order of magnitude | edits are ranked in seconds; only the chosen edit is evaluated | whether the saved compute is better spent on more search than on the readout |
| 3 | Huxley-Godel Machine selection (Thompson sampling on clade counts) is a good parent picker | used throughout the reported runs | an ablation against greedy or other tree policies at the same budget |

**Laptop adaptation preserves:** steering/fit/held-out splits; 8 diff coordinates read from a real unified diff; ridge readout; HGM Thompson sampling on clade counts.

**Laptop adaptation changes:** programs are cipher breakers rendered from templates, not GPT-4o patches; 48/96 ciphers, not HumanEval+; labels are fit-split letter accuracy.


## SO-RSI: second-order problem solving for RSI (arXiv:2610.05701)

| # | Claim | Supporting evidence (as summarised) | What remains untested |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnosing why failures occur before patching beats first-order patching | Lean 4: +21.8 points held-out; Verus: +25.8 points over Naive RSI | domains without formal verifiers; budgets where probes are expensive |
| 2 | Structural anomalies (recurrence, opposing edits, expectation mismatch) are reliable triggers | behavioural analysis shows reduced thrashing | false-positive rate of the triggers; sensitivity to window sizes |
| 3 | Inquiry memory carries causal evidence across rounds | reported as part of the full system | a memory-off ablation isolating its contribution |

**Laptop adaptation preserves:** the three anomaly detectors; probes that test a causal hypothesis (fitness of the true key vs the found key); inquiry memory that re-weights the proposer; identical proposer and acceptance rule in both arms.

**Laptop adaptation changes:** probes are hand-written for this domain; the proposer is a stochastic symptom-driven policy, not an LLM; 40 evaluations, not 24 hours.


## SwarmRSI: evolving scientific agent swarms (arXiv:2610.20431)

| # | Claim | Supporting evidence (as summarised) | What remains untested |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Feedback-guided swarm mutation plus complementary recombination reaches SOTA on research benchmarks | OpenProblems, NatureBench, MLE-Bench Lite results | matched-compute comparison against a single strong agent |
| 2 | Accumulated swarm experience improves later experiments | experience-impact analysis | how much of the gain is diversity versus extra compute |

**Laptop adaptation preserves:** five agents with distinct research directions; shared experience table; module-wise recombination of same-generation improvements; recursive re-weighting of each agent's proposal distribution.

**Laptop adaptation changes:** agents are policies over an edit vocabulary, not LLM scientists; one task family; 40 evaluations.
